What can you lose while planning everything yourself?

Travellers who plan every part separately often run into these.

Time

Time goes into multiple bookings and follow-ups.

Avoidable cost

Arranging each service on its own can make costs harder to plan.

Peace of mind

Constant coordination and follow-up before you even leave.

Family Time

Worrying about arrangements instead of enjoying time with your family.

Travel Experience

Unplanned transport or sightseeing can take away from the experience.
